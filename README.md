# My Favorite Movie: Spider-Man: No Way Home

## About the Movie

**Spider-Man: No Way Home** is a superhero movie released in **2021** and directed by **Jon Watts**. It combines *action, adventure, and emotional moments*.

After Peter Parker’s identity is revealed, he asks Doctor Strange for help. However, a spell goes wrong and brings visitors from other universes into his world.

## Main Characters

- **Peter Parker / Spider-Man** — Tom Holland
- **MJ** — Zendaya
- **Ned Leeds** — Jacob Batalon
- **Doctor Strange** — Benedict Cumberbatch

## Why It Is My Favorite

1. The action scenes are exciting.
2. Peter’s friendship with MJ and Ned makes the story meaningful.
3. The movie shows the difficult choices that come with being a hero.
4. Familiar characters make the movie memorable.

### My Reflection

> Being a hero means accepting responsibility and helping others, even when it requires personal sacrifice.

Peter’s experiences remind me that our decisions affect the people around us. His story shows the importance of **friendship, courage, and responsibility**.
